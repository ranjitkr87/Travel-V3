## Detailed Tour Specifications
1. **Munnar & The Cardamom Hills**
   - Duration: 3 Days / 2 Nights
   - Region: High Range, Western Ghats (1,600m altitude)
   - Base Price: ₹14,200 (Includes Private Chauffeur + Estate Stay)
   - Highlights: Colonial cart paths, boutique organic tea factories, shaded cardamom spice groves, Lockhart Gap mist viewpoints, heirloom bungalow stays.
2. **Vembanad & Alleppey Houseboat**
   - Duration: 2 Nights / 3 Days
   - Region: Vembanad Lake, Alleppey (Alappuzha), Kumarakom
   - Base Price: ₹18,500 (Full Board · Private Chef)
   - Highlights: Exclusive anjili-wood kettuvallam barge, palm-sheltered lagoons, secluded village jetties, fresh Karimeen (pearl spot) prepared onboard.
3. **Wayanad Rainforest & Coffee**
   - Duration: 4 Days / 3 Nights
   - Region: Northern Western Ghats, Wayanad
   - Base Price: ₹19,800
   - Highlights: Dense emerald canopy walks, Edakkal prehistoric caves, wild bamboo thickets, heirloom Arabica/Robusta coffee estate stays.
4. **Old Fort Kochi & Mattancherry**
   - Duration: Single Day (Full Day Cultural Drift)
   - Region: Fort Kochi & Mattancherry Peninsula
   - Base Price: ₹3,400
   - Highlights: Mattancherry Dutch Palace, Paradesi Synagogue, Jew Town spice godowns, St. Francis Church, cantilevered Chinese fishing nets at dusk.
5. **Marari Coastline & Village Drift**
   - Duration: 2 Days / 1 Night
   - Region: Mararikulam, Arabian Sea Coast
   - Base Price: ₹8,900
   - Highlights: Fisherfolk hamlets, secluded golden sand beaches, coir-weaving co-operatives, coastal back-road cycling.
